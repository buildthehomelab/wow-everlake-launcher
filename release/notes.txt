Regular launcher update for Windows and macOS, delivered through the launcher's own updater.

Adds every profession to the Everlake addon the launcher installs. Everlake lets a character learn all primary professions, but the game's own profession window and Professions tab only have room for two. The addon now adds the rest as tabs down the side of the profession window, in the same column as the game's own tabs, starting at the top and shrinking to fit when you know many; a click opens that profession. The game's "Professions" overview tab is hidden. A small panel beside the spellbook lists them too, with their ranks. /everlake prof shows or hides the panel, and /everlake prof book stops it opening with the spellbook.

Validation: Windows, Linux and macOS test suites. The panel and the side tabs were tried in game on WoW Forever 1.60.1.71091 with a test copy of the addon: every profession opened from its tab. The tabs' artwork still differs slightly from the game's own.

Windows executables are unsigned; the Mac app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. Both platforms and SHA256SUMS.txt are included.
