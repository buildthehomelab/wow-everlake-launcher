Regular launcher update for Windows and macOS, delivered through the launcher's own updater.

The Everlake addon's Professions page now lists Cooking, Fishing and First Aid in the same scrolling list as the primary professions, each as a card in the game's style with its crafting window's picture, its spells (Bait and Tackle included) and its bar. Each profession's bar plays its own animation (learned from the game the first time that profession's bar or crafting window shows), ranks come from the game (Apprentice, Journeyman), the profession's own spell comes first, and the bar's spark no longer sticks out at low rank. The red X to unlearn a profession is gone: every profession can be learned on Everlake, and a trainer still unlearns.

Validation: Windows, Linux and macOS test suites, runs of the addon against stand-in game frames, and the page tried in game with a test copy of the addon.

Windows executables are unsigned; the Mac app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. Both platforms and SHA256SUMS.txt are included.
